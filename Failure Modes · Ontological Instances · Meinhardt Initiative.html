Acknowledgement as Closure
Stage 1 of 5
FailureMode :: Procedural Termination
:AcknowledgementAsClosure · #AcknowledgementAsClosure
A formal acknowledgement stands in for substantive processing and ends the procedural chain without a determination. The output does not depend on the office or the person: an individually signed acknowledgement produced the same result as a form one.

Interpretation
Failure modes are repeatable outputs of institutional processes, not isolated anomalies. The chain, stated by level:
Process —ignores→ Submission
Process —producesFailure→ Failure mode —triggers→ Legal condition
Process —violates→ Right
The five modes run in order, and stage 5 resets to stage 1. Legal conditions here are matters such as failure of institutional disposition (no determination issued) and Art. 40.3 Bunreacht fair-procedures grounds (O'Keeffe, Meadows). Art. 41 CFR is addressed to EU institutions and is not a target.

Navigation runs both ways by link: each mode links to its relations, and each relation on the Relations page links back here by anchor. Inverse properties are not yet declared in the ontology.
